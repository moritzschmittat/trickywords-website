Help and support

Write to support@trickywords.uk and we will reply as soon as we can.

A daily session

Tap "Start today's practice". Read each word aloud and let your child write it down. Check the spelling, then tap Correct or Incorrect. After a miss, show your child the word and ask them to copy it out. The app tells you when you are done.

How words become Mastered

A new word is tried once. If your child already knows it, it comes back a week later and is then Mastered. Otherwise it is learned in the session and tested again the next day, then after 3, 7 and 14 days. Two quiet checks follow, after one month and three more. A miss sends a word back a little, never to the start.

Adding words

In Words, tap "Add my own" and type or paste words, one per line. "Add from library" gives you lists for each school year in three levels. The switch at the top of Words chooses what to practise: Own words, Both, or App Library.

A second screen

Tap "Second screen" to show a code. On another phone or tablet, open Tricky Words, tap "Use as a screen" and scan it. Both devices need an internet connection.

A laptop, TV or classroom board works too: open trickywords.uk/screen in its browser and type the code. Nothing needs installing.

Keeping progress safe

Progress is stored on your phone only. In Settings, "Back up all children" saves it to a file you can keep, and "Restore from a backup" brings it back on this phone or a new one.

Updating from an older version

Version 2 brings your children, words and progress across automatically the first time it opens. If something is missing, do not add a new child. Write to us and we will help.
