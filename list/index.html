Your school's spelling list

Your child's teacher sets the weekly spelling words in Tricky Words, and they arrive on your phone.

Your code: see your teacher's note

In the Tricky Words app, open Words, choose "Words from school", type this code and add the list. Each week's words then arrive on their own.

It only goes one way: the school sends words and nothing is sent back. Can't see "Words from school"? Update the app to version 2.1 or later.
