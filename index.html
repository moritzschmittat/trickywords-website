How a session works

You hold the phone. Your child holds the pencil.

  1. 1

    You read the word

    The app shows it to you, not to your child.

  2. 2

    They write it down

    On paper, the way they will in the spelling test.

  3. 3

    You tap right or wrong

    The app does the rest and says when you are done.

Every word comes back just before it fades

You never have to decide which words to repeat. The app picks them for you. Words are tested again after longer and longer gaps. Pass the last test and the word is Mastered. A miss sends a word back a little, never to the start.

The teacher's words, straight to your phone

If your child's school uses Tricky Words, type the class code from the teacher's note once. Each week's spelling words then arrive on their own, on the day the teacher sets, so your phone always has the latest list. No printout to hunt for in the book bag.

It only goes one way. The school sends words out and your phone receives them. Nothing goes back: the school cannot see who uses the list, or how any child is doing.

Misses get fixed straight away

After a miss, show your child the word and ask them to copy it out. It comes back a few words later, in the same session, until it sticks.
