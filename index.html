Spelling practice, sorted

Tricky Words replaces spelling flashcards with a short daily routine on your phone. Five to ten minutes a day.

Get it on the App Store

Free for early adopters: download before 31 March 2027 and everything in the app today stays free for you, for good. No adverts, no accounts. An Android version is on its way.

You read. They write.

Read the word aloud, let your child write it down, then tap Correct or Incorrect. The app decides which words to ask and when, and tells you when you are done.

Misses get fixed straight away

After a miss, show your child the word and ask them to copy it out. It comes back a few words later, in the same session, until it sticks.

Tested again at the right time

Every word comes back after 1, 3, 7 and 14 days. Pass the 14-day test and the word is Mastered. A miss sends a word back a little, never to the start.

Word lists for Reception to Year 6

Nearly 900 words in Easy, Medium and Difficult lists. Or add the school's own spelling list by typing or pasting it, one word per line.

A clear finish every day

No endless sessions. When today's words are done, the app says so, and brings the right ones back tomorrow.

A second screen

Show each word on another phone or tablet after you mark it, with a fanfare for a correct answer. Scan a code to join.
