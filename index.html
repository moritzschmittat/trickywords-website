Spelling practice, sorted

Tricky Words replaces spelling flashcards with a short daily routine on your phone. Five to ten minutes a day.

Get it on the App Store

Free. No adverts, no subscriptions, no accounts. An Android version is on its way.

How it works

You read the word aloud, your child writes it down, and you tap Correct or Incorrect. The app decides which words to ask and when.

  • Tested again at the right time. Every word comes back after 1, 3, 7 and 14 days. Pass the 14-day test and the word is Mastered.
  • A clear finish every day. The app picks today's words and tells you when you are done.
  • Misses get fixed straight away. Your child copies the word out and tries again a few words later.
  • Word lists for Reception to Year 6. Nearly 900 words in Easy, Medium and Difficult lists, or add the school's own list.
  • A second screen. Show each word on another phone or tablet by scanning a code.
