Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026. This policy covers the Tricky Words app (version 2.0 and later) and this website.

In short

  • Your children's names, your own word lists and their progress stay on your phone.
  • The app shares anonymous practice results to help improve it. You are told about this when you first open the app, and you can switch it off in Settings at any time.
  • There are no accounts, no adverts and no tracking across other apps or websites.

Who we are

Tricky Words is made by Schmittat Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 9452378), which is the data controller. Schmittat Ltd is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office. Contact: support@trickywords.uk.

What stays on your phone

The following is stored only on your device and is never sent to us:

  • each child's first name and school year
  • the words you add yourself
  • which words each child has practised and how far along they are
  • your settings

A backup file you create in Settings contains this information. It goes wherever you choose to save or send it, and not to us.

Anonymous practice results

To improve the word lists and the timing of reviews, the app sends a record of each answer to our server. This is on by default. Nothing is sent until you have seen the notice in the app, and you can switch it off under Settings, "Help improve Tricky Words".

What is sentDetail
A random install numberCreated by the app. It is not your Apple or Google account, device serial number or advertising identifier.
A random number for each childSo that results from one child can be told apart from another's. Never the child's name.
School yearThe year you chose for the child, if any.
The word, for App Library wordsFor words you added yourself, only the number of letters is sent, never the word.
The resultCorrect or incorrect, the stage the word was at, how many days since it was last practised, and the date and time.
App version and platformFor example "2.0.0, iOS".

We cannot tell from this who you or your child are, and we do not try to. We use it only to see which words are hard and whether the review schedule works. We do not sell it or share it with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in improving the app, balanced by the notice and the switch described above. Because the records carry no names or contact details, we normally cannot find the records that belong to a particular family. If you want yours removed, write to us and we will explain how to find the install number so we can delete them.

Second screen

If you use "Second screen", the word being practised and its result are relayed over the internet to the devices that scanned your code, so that they can show it. This is not stored on our server. The camera is used only to scan the code. No pictures are saved or sent.

Other connections the app makes

The app downloads updates to the App Library word lists from our server. As with any internet connection, the server sees your IP address while it answers. We do not use it to identify you.

The optional daily reminder is created on your phone. It does not go through our server.

Where the data is held

Our server is provided by Supabase and is located in London, United Kingdom. Supabase acts on our instructions as a data processor.

How long we keep it

Anonymous practice results are kept for as long as they are useful for improving the app, and are reviewed at least once a year.

Children

Tricky Words is designed to be operated by a parent or other adult while a child writes on paper. The app does not ask children for any information and does not contain adverts or links to social networks.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted by GitHub, which records visitors' IP addresses in its server logs for security purposes. If you email us, we keep your message for as long as we need it to help you.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you, and you can object to our use of it. Write to support@trickywords.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
