Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026. This policy covers the Tricky Words app (version 2.1 and later) and this website.

In short

  • Your children's names, your own word lists and their progress stay on your phone.
  • The app sends us nothing about you, your children or how they practise.
  • If your school shares its spelling lists, the words come from the school to your phone. Nothing goes back to the school or to us.
  • There are no accounts, no adverts and no tracking across other apps or websites.

Who we are

Tricky Words is made by Schmittat Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 9452378), which is the data controller. Schmittat Ltd is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office. Contact: support@trickywords.uk.

What stays on your phone

The following is stored only on your device and is never sent to us:

  • each child's first name and school year
  • the words you add yourself
  • which words each child has practised and how far along they are
  • your settings

A backup file you create in Settings contains this information. It goes wherever you choose to save or send it, and not to us.

Words from school

If your child's school uses Tricky Words, you can type or scan the school's list code. Your phone then asks our server for the words on that list and checks now and then for new ones. This goes one way only: words come from the school to your phone. The app sends nothing back. The school cannot see who uses its list, how many families use it, or how any child is doing, and neither can we.

The code you entered and the words you received are stored on your phone. You can remove a school list in the app at any time.

Second screen

If you use "Second screen", the word being practised and its result are relayed over the internet to the devices that scanned your code, so that they can show it. This is not stored on our server. The camera is used only to scan the code. No pictures are saved or sent.

Other connections the app makes

The app downloads updates to the App Library word lists, and any school lists you have added, from our server. As with any internet connection, the server sees your IP address while it answers. We do not use it to identify you and we do not keep a record of which phone asked for which list.

The optional daily reminder is created on your phone. It does not go through our server.

Teachers and schools (preview)

School staff can sign in at trickywords.uk/teach to keep spelling lists for their school. To do that we store:

  • the email addresses on a school's staff list. A colleague may add a member of staff's work address so that they can sign in. An address is used only to send the sign-in link and code and to recognise who may open the school's lists. Our lawful basis is that we need it to provide the service the teacher asked for;
  • the school's name and the lists its staff create: a list name, the words, and the dates the words go out. Lists should not contain children's names.

Anyone who has a list's code can read that list's name and words. Parents who use a code do not need an account and we do not record who they are. Staying signed in uses storage in the teacher's own browser, not advertising or tracking cookies. Staff can delete a list, remove an address from the staff list, or delete their own account from the page at any time, and it is removed from our server straight away.

Where the data is held

Our server is provided by Supabase and is located in London, United Kingdom. Supabase acts on our instructions as a data processor.

How long we keep it

A teacher's email address and a school's lists are kept until a member of staff deletes them. We hold nothing about families, so there is nothing to keep.

Children

Tricky Words is designed to be operated by a parent or other adult while a child writes on paper. The app does not ask children for any information and does not contain adverts or links to social networks.

This website

The second screen page at trickywords.uk/screen connects to our server to receive the word being practised from your phone, so that it can show it. Nothing from that page is stored.

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted by GitHub, which records visitors' IP addresses in its server logs for security purposes. If you email us, we keep your message for as long as we need it to help you.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you, and you can object to our use of it. Write to support@trickywords.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
