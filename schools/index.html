How it works

Three steps, and the first one is the only one that takes any time.

  1. 1

    Type in the words

    One week at a time, or paste the whole term and let each week go out on its own date.

  2. 2

    Hand out one code

    Each class has a code and a QR code. Print the ready-made note for parents and send it home once.

  3. 3

    Families have the words

    New words arrive in the Tricky Words app each week. No printout to lose on the way home.

Ready to use. The school tool is open, and the free app that receives your lists is on the App Store. Families need version 2.1 or later.

A whole term in one sitting

Paste every week's words with an empty line between weeks. They go out a week apart, with the spelling test date beside them. Move any date afterwards, for half term or a trip.

One place for the whole school

Every class's lists live in one shared space. Add colleagues by email address and they see the same lists. There are no passwords: each person signs in with a link sent to their own inbox.

Printouts when you want them

Print any week as a practice sheet, or as slips to cut out and stick into writing books. The words are typed once and used everywhere.

And on the classroom board

Open trickywords.uk/screen on the board and it shows each word once the class has written it, big enough to read from the back row. Nothing to install.

  • From any phoneThe board shows the words sent from a phone running the app. Its code stays the same, so the board can keep the page bookmarked.
  • Classroom modeSay the word, show it, then tap how many got it right: few, half or most. The board answers each time.

One way only: words go out, nothing comes back

The school publishes its spelling words. Families receive them. That is the whole exchange, so a school never holds information about a child or a family.

No pupil accounts

Children have no logins and no passwords. There is nothing for teachers to set up or reset.

No pupil data

The school's space holds staff email addresses and spelling words. It never holds a child's name or results.

Nothing is sent back

A parent types the class code into the app, which fetches the words. The school cannot see who uses a list, how many families do, or how any child is getting on.

Free for every family

The app has no adverts and no charges for families, so no child is left out.

Questions schools ask

What does it cost?

Families never pay. A school can use it free for its first term. After that there is one yearly price for the whole school, covering every class and every member of staff.

Can we see which families use it, or how pupils are doing?

No, and that is deliberate. Words travel one way, from the school to the family's phone. Practice results stay on that phone and are not sent to the school or to us.

What do families need?

An iPhone or iPad with the free Tricky Words app. An Android version is on its way. For a family without either, print that week's words as a sheet or a slip.

What do staff need?

A web browser and their own email address. Whoever sets the school up adds colleagues by email, and they receive an invitation.

Does a child use the phone?

No. The parent holds the phone and the child writes on paper, the way they will in the spelling test.

What happens if we stop?

Families keep every word they already have and can carry on practising. New words stop going out.

Can we delete everything?

Yes. Staff can delete a list, remove a colleague or delete their own account from the page, and it is removed from our server straight away. See the privacy policy.
